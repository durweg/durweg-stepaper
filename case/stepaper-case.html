Lessons

Reading papers is a headache, and dropping the PDF on an AI does not work well either, because the AI also outputs a mass of information that is hard to understand. I turned the way I understand papers into a durweg workflow: start from the core problem, look step by step at how the researchers did it, and ask back: was this core problem solved? I think it works very well.

The method

My method: read a paper and turn it into group-meeting slides

Task

Read a paper, understand its core claim, and summarize it as slides to present at the group meeting.

How I do it

1. Read the abstract first and find the paper's core claim: which concrete problem it solves.

2. Read on step by step (cause → method → why it works → how it was tested → results), asking questions and trying to refute at every step.

3. Retell the core claim in my own words, best as a comparison (is A or B better?).

4. Judge: did the paper solve its problem? How strong is the evidence (my reservations)? Did I reach my own goal (understanding the paper)?

5. Outline the slides: each slide answers one question, and its title is that slide's one-sentence conclusion. Suggested order:

  • Slide 1: title + the core claim in one sentence (the concrete problem the paper solves)
  • Slide 2: where the problem comes from (cause)
  • Slide 3: their method
  • Slide 4: why it works
  • Slide 5: how it was tested (data, comparison set-up)
  • Slide 6: results, one figure or table showing the core comparison (A vs B), with metric and numbers
  • Slide 7: my questions and judgement (solved or not, strength of evidence, reservations)
  • Slide 8: my retelling (one sentence) + questions for the group

6. Build the slides from the outline: key figures from the paper, little text per slide, no piles of formulas.

7. Rehearse once: does every slide answer its question, and do the slides form one line?

Where it goes wrong

  • Misreading the paper's purpose (for example, taking "accuracy does not drop at another hospital" for "higher accuracy").
  • Only retelling the paper without asking or refuting, so it stays unclear whether the problem was solved.
  • Too much text and too many formulas on a slide; one slide covering several things.
  • Result numbers without the metric, the comparison and the source.

Where I decide

  • Whether my understanding of the core claim is right.
  • How strong the evidence is and what my reservations are.
  • The slide outline (which slides, in which order).

经验

读论文是一件头疼的事情，把PDF丢给AI也效果并不好，因为AI同样会输出大量难以理解的信息。我把我理解论文的方式做成了一个durweg工作流：从核心问题出发，一步一步看研究人员是怎么做的，并且反问，这个核心问题是否得到了解决？我认为效果很好。

方法

我的方法：读论文并做成组会 PPT

Task

读一篇论文，看懂它的核心论点，并把它总结成 PPT，在组会上报告。

How I do it

1. 先看摘要，找到论文的核心论点：它要解决什么具体问题。

2. 一步一步往下读（原因 → 办法 → 为什么有用 → 怎么检验 → 结果），每一步都提问、尝试反驳。

3. 用自己的话复述核心论点，最好说成一个对比（A 和 B 哪个更好）。

4. 判断：论文的问题解决了吗？证据有多强（我的保留）？我自己的目的（看懂这篇论文）达到了吗？

5. 列 PPT 大纲：每页只回答一个问题，标题就是这一页的一句话结论。建议顺序：

  • 第 1 页：题目 + 一句话核心论点（论文要解决的具体问题）
  • 第 2 页：问题从哪里来（原因）
  • 第 3 页：他们的办法
  • 第 4 页：为什么有用
  • 第 5 页：怎么检验（数据、对比设置）
  • 第 6 页：结果，用一张图或表展示核心对比（A 和 B），写清指标和数字
  • 第 7 页：我的追问和判断（解决了没有、证据强弱、保留）
  • 第 8 页：我的复述（一句话）+ 想在组会上讨论的问题

6. 按大纲做页面：从论文里取关键图，每页文字少，不堆公式。

7. 自己试讲一遍：每一页是否回答了它的问题，前后能否连成一条线。

Where it goes wrong

  • 误解论文的目的（例如把"换医院后准确率不掉"误当成"提高准确率"）。
  • 只复述论文，不提问、不反驳，看不出问题到底解决了没有。
  • PPT 文字和公式太多，一页讲好几件事。
  • 结果数字没写清指标、对比对象和出处。

Where I decide

  • 我对核心论点的理解是否正确。
  • 证据强不强、我的保留是什么。
  • PPT 大纲（讲哪些页、按什么顺序）。
